## Features

**Weather**
- Current weather for your location or any city in the world
- Hourly forecast for the next 24 hours and a 6-day forecast
- Tap any day to see rain, wind, UV, sunrise, sunset and daylight
- Wind speed, direction, gusts and the Beaufort scale
- Humidity, dew point, cloud cover, visibility and pressure
- Air quality (US and European AQI, PM2.5, PM10, ozone and more)
- Pollen levels (Europe) and moon phase
- Celsius or Fahrenheit

**Explore**
- Today's 5 strongest earthquakes (USGS)
- Art of the Day from The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York
- On This Day in history (Wikipedia)

**Also**
- Save your favorite cities
- Four animated icon styles
