Kalu Weather is a simple, beautiful weather app with animated icons.

<b>Weather</b>
<ul>
<li>Current weather for your location or any city in the world</li>
<li>Hourly forecast for the next 24 hours and a 6-day forecast</li>
<li>Tap any day to see rain, wind, UV, sunrise, sunset and daylight</li>
<li>Wind speed, direction, gusts and the Beaufort scale</li>
<li>Humidity, dew point, cloud cover, visibility and pressure</li>
<li>Air quality (US and European AQI, PM2.5, PM10, ozone and more)</li>
<li>Pollen levels (Europe)</li>
<li>Moon phase</li>
<li>Celsius or Fahrenheit</li>
</ul>

<b>Explore</b>
<ul>
<li>Today's 5 strongest earthquakes (USGS)</li>
<li>Art of the Day from The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York</li>
<li>On This Day in history (Wikipedia)</li>
</ul>

<b>Also</b>
<ul>
<li>Save your favorite cities</li>
<li>Four animated icon styles</li>
<li>No ads, no tracking, no account</li>
</ul>
